Frequently Asked

Frequently answered questions.

Everything you need to know about ElectroShorts, system requirements, and our lifetime license model.

Is ₹4,900 really a one-time payment with no subscription?

Yes. You pay ₹4,900 INR once and receive a lifetime license key for the desktop app. There are no monthly fees, no hidden export credits, for the full year. only basic charges will apply from next year.

What are the recommended Windows system requirements?

ElectroShorts runs on Windows 10/11 (64-bit). For the fastest transcription and 60fps rendering, we recommend an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 1050ti or newer (or Intel 11th Gen+ CPU with QuickSync) and 8GB of RAM.

Do my videos ever get uploaded to an external server?

No. The core transcription engine and the speaker tracking computer vision models run 100% locally on your machine. Your raw video footage never leaves your hard drive.

Can I install it on more than one computer?

Yes! Every license allows activation on up to 2 personal Windows PCs simultaneously (e.g. your main editing desktop and your travel laptop).

What video formats and aspect ratios are supported?

Input: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, and ProRes up to 4K 60fps. Output: High-bitrate 9:16 vertical, 1:1 Feed Post ,16:9 Post MP4s optimized for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

Is there a trial available for new users?

Yes, we offer trials for new creators and studios. Please contact our support team via email with your studio credentials and production details to receive evaluation access.
